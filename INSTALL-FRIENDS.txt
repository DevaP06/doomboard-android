🌀 *Doomboard* – counts how many Instagram Reels you scroll + a leaderboard to see who's the most cooked 🧟

📥 *Download:*
https://github.com/DevaP06/doomboard-android/releases/latest/download/doomboard.apk

*How to install (2 min):*
1️⃣ Open the link, download, then tap the file to install.
   • If asked, allow "Install unknown apps" for Chrome/WhatsApp/Files.
   • Samsung: if it says *blocked by Auto Blocker* → Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker → off. Install, then turn it back on.
   • If Play Protect warns → *More details* → *Install anyway*. (It warns because the app isn't on the Play Store yet.)

2️⃣ Android 13 or newer: Settings → Apps → *Doomboard* → ⋮ (top right) → *Allow restricted settings*.

3️⃣ Open Doomboard → *Turn on counting* → *Agree & open settings* → tap *Doomboard* → switch it ON.

4️⃣ Pick a nickname and *Join leaderboard* (or stay private, your choice).

Now just use Instagram Reels like normal 😅 A little counter pops up while you scroll.

🔒 It only counts swipes inside Instagram. It can't see your messages, captions or what you watch, and nothing leaves your phone unless you join the leaderboard.
