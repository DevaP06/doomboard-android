Install on Android

Doomboard isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install it straight from our GitHub. Takes about 2 minutes.

Download the app

Download Doomboard (.apk), then open the file. If asked, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser.

Turn on counting

Open Doomboard → Turn on counting → Agree & open settings → tap Doomboard → switch it on.

Switch greyed out or "Restricted setting"? (Android 13+)

That's normal for apps from outside the Play Store. Go to Settings → Apps → Doomboard → ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings, then switch counting on again. The ⋮ option only appears after you've tried once.

Scroll like normal 😅

Open Instagram Reels. The counter pops up as you swipe. Join the leaderboard whenever you're ready.

🛡️ Seeing "App blocked to protect your device"?

That's Google's anti-scam filter. It blocks any downloaded app that uses Accessibility (the feature Doomboard needs to notice your swipes) unless it comes from the Play Store. Two ways around it:

  • Obtainium (free, open-source app installer): install Obtainium, tap Add App, paste github.com/DevaP06/doomboard-android, then Install. You also get update alerts.
  • Pause Play Protect for a minute: Play Store → profile picture → Play Protect → ⚙️ → turn off "Scan apps", install Doomboard, then turn it straight back on. Only do this for apps you trust.

📥 Installing

"App blocked to protect your device"

Google's anti-scam filter blocks downloaded apps that use Accessibility, and there's no "Install anyway" button. Fixes, easiest first:

  1. Obtainium: install Obtainium (free, open source) → Add App → paste github.com/DevaP06/doomboard-android → Install.
  2. Pause Play Protect: Play Store → profile picture → Play Protect → ⚙️ → turn off Scan apps with Play Protect → install Doomboard → turn it back on straight away.
  3. Install from a laptop with a USB cable (ask whoever sent you the app).
"Unsafe app blocked" / "Harmful app" warning with "More details"

Tap More details → Install anyway. Play Protect warns about any app it hasn't seen on the Play Store before.

"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps"

Tap Settings on that message → turn on Allow from this source for the app you downloaded with (Chrome, WhatsApp, Files…) → go back and tap Install.

Samsung: "Blocked by Auto Blocker"

Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker → turn it off → install Doomboard → turn Auto Blocker back on. Doomboard keeps working with it on.

"App not installed" or "conflicts with an existing package"

You have an older test version signed differently. Uninstall Doomboard first, then install again. This resets your counts on that phone.

"There was a problem parsing the package"

The download didn't finish, or your phone is older than Android 8.0. Delete the file, download it again on a stable connection, and check Settings → About phone → Android version.

🔐 Turning on counting

The Doomboard switch is greyed out, or says "Restricted setting"

Android 13+ locks Accessibility for apps from outside the Play Store. Try to switch Doomboard on once (you'll see the "Restricted setting" message), then go to Settings → Apps → Doomboard → ⋮ (top right) → Allow restricted settings → confirm with your PIN or fingerprint. Now switch it on again.

I can't find Doomboard in Accessibility settings

Look under Installed apps or Downloaded apps inside Settings → Accessibility (on Samsung: Settings → Accessibility → Installed apps). Or open Doomboard and tap Turn on counting, which takes you to the right screen.

Is it safe to give Accessibility access?

Doomboard only receives events from the Instagram app and only uses them to notice a swipe to a new Reel. It never reads captions, messages or usernames, never records your screen, and does nothing in other apps. You can switch it off any time in the same settings screen.

🔢 Counting & pop-up

The count isn't going up
  1. Open Doomboard: it should say ✅ Counting Reels in Instagram. If it says counting is off, turn it on again.
  2. Make sure you're in Instagram's Reels tab or a full-screen Reel. Feed posts and Stories don't count.
  3. Still nothing? Switch Doomboard off and on in Accessibility settings, or restart your phone.
  4. If Instagram just updated, its layout may have changed. Let us know on GitHub.
Counting keeps turning itself off (common on Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo)

Battery savers put apps to sleep and stop their services. Let Doomboard stay awake:

  • Any phone: Settings → Apps → Doomboard → Battery → Unrestricted.
  • Samsung: Settings → Battery (or Battery and device care) → Background usage limits → Never sleeping apps → add Doomboard.
  • Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO: Settings → Apps → Doomboard → Battery saver → No restrictions, and turn on Autostart.
I don't see the pop-up counter

Check that Show a pop-up counter while scrolling Reels is ticked in Doomboard. The pop-up appears only when you swipe to a new Reel and fades after 4 seconds.

No limit alerts

Set a daily limit in Doomboard, then allow notifications: Settings → Apps → Doomboard → Notifications → on. Alerts come at 80%, at 100%, then every 20 reels over.

The count seems a bit off

Swiping back to a previous Reel counts it again, and Reels opened from DMs count too. It's built to be close, not perfect.

🏆 Leaderboard

"couldn't load" on the leaderboard

Check your internet, then close and reopen Doomboard. If it's still down, the server may be waking up. Try again in a few minutes.

"That name is taken" or "Let's keep it friendly"

Nicknames are unique and filtered. Tap 🎲 for a random one, or add a number to yours.

How do I leave the leaderboard?

In Doomboard tap leave & delete my data. Your nickname and scores are deleted from the server. Your counts on your phone stay.

🔄 Updating & removing

How do I update?

Installed with Obtainium? It tells you and updates in one tap. Otherwise, download from the same link and tap Update. Your counts and nickname stay. You may need to pause Play Protect again for that minute.

How do I uninstall?

If you joined the leaderboard, first tap leave & delete my data in the app so your server data is removed too. Then uninstall Doomboard like any app.

Still stuck? Open an issue on GitHub with your phone model and a screenshot.

FAQ

Can Doomboard see my messages or what I watch?

No. It only works inside the Instagram app and only notices when you swipe to a new Reel, from the screen layout. It never reads captions, messages, usernames or what you watch, and it doesn't record your screen.

What gets shared if I join the leaderboard?

Only your chosen nickname, a random anonymous ID and your daily totals (reels and minutes). If you don't join, nothing leaves your phone. "Leave & delete my data" in the app removes you completely.

Why does it need Accessibility?

Android has no other way for an app to notice swipes inside another app. Doomboard uses it only for Instagram and only to count. The app explains this before you turn it on, and you can switch it off any time in Settings.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Apple doesn't let apps see what happens inside other apps, so per-Reel counting isn't possible on iPhone.

Does it work with TikTok or YouTube Shorts?

Right now it's Instagram Reels only. More apps may come later.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions. It's a solo side project.
